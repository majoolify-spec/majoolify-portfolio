## Context

Prompt products often fail when they expose too much raw model behavior and not enough structure. The challenge here was to make an interface that still felt flexible for power users, while giving the workflow enough shape to stay usable over time.

## Approach

I treated the product less like a chat surface and more like an authoring environment:

- reusable prompt blocks,
- clearer content hierarchy,
- and interface affordances that made prompt composition feel intentional instead of improvised.

That framing also influenced the admin model. The content system had to be editable, bilingual, and maintainable without destroying the clarity of the frontend.

## Outcome

The resulting direction turned AI generation into something closer to a guided studio experience. That mattered because it made the product easier to operate, easier to explain, and easier to extend without rewriting the core interaction model each time.

## Why it belongs in this portfolio

This project sits at the intersection of my two strongest lanes:

- frontend design execution,
- and AI prompt workflow design that respects product reality.
